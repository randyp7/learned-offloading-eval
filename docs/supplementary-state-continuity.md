# Supplementary RQ2 analyses

The main paper reports the matched warm/cold evaluation of the warm-trained policies. This document provides the complete paired-window audit, the night-condition results and the exploratory cold-training analysis. The construction of the window pairs is described in `warm-cold-protocol.md`.

All tables below are generated by `analysis/make_supplementary.py` from the files named in each section.

## Exploratory cold-training analysis

The cold-training experiment is supplementary because the main RQ2 result concerns evaluation-window state continuity.

The cold-trained arm trains DQN-Full and DQN-NoV2V on 20-frame cold-start episodes, reproducing the earlier study's training protocol (`configs/cold_training.yaml`, `scripts/slurm/train_coldtrain.sh`). Its ten checkpoints (`checkpoints/berlin_coldtrain_{v2v,local}_seed10{0..4}`) and the ten warm-trained checkpoints (`checkpoints/berlin_general_{v2v,local}_seed10{0..4}`) are evaluated on the same paired windows.

Source files:

- `results/eval_coldtrain_paired_<warm|cold>_<period>.csv` (cold-trained policies) and `results/eval_paired_<warm|cold>_<period>.csv` (warm-trained policies);
- `results/coldtrain_density/coldtrain_density_frames.csv` (vehicles per frame in the cold training episodes and in the warm trajectory at the same times);
- `results/peer_audit/eval_peer_availability_coldtrain_paired_<warm|cold>_<period>.csv`.

### Principal findings

- Cold-trained DQN-Full has a lower mean than warm-trained DQN-Full in every loaded sample, under both warm and cold evaluation.
- The five-run comparisons are inconclusive (Welch p = 0.06–0.32 in the loaded samples; 0.09 and 0.21 for the seed-first loaded aggregates).
- Cold-trained policies have 1.6–3.4 times greater between-run SD in the loaded samples and the full day.
- DQN-NoV2V changes little between training regimes.
- All interaction confidence intervals include zero.
- Cold-trained DQN-Full selects V2V more often and, in the warm windows, meets fewer V2V deadlines; these behavioural differences are descriptive rather than evidence of a systematic population-level training effect.

### Scope of the cold-training comparison

The cold-trained arm retains the earlier study's:

- cold-start episode initialisation;
- 20-frame episode duration;
- observation space;
- action space;
- deadline-only reward weights; and
- learner configuration.

The present experiment differs in training budget and overall network scenario. It therefore tests one potential explanation for the earlier result rather than constituting a complete replication.

Across the loaded evaluation samples, cold-trained DQN-Full has lower mean deadline satisfaction and greater between-run variability. However:

- individual Welch tests are inconclusive;
- loaded-period aggregate comparisons are inconclusive;
- policy-by-training-regime confidence intervals contain zero; and
- behavioural differences are reported descriptively.

Additional independent runs would improve precision, but are not assumed to produce statistical significance.
