# Supplementary RQ1 analyses

The main paper reports the principal deadline-satisfaction result and a condensed account of peer availability and V2V effectiveness. This document provides the complete mechanism and robustness analyses.

All tables below are generated by `analysis/make_supplementary.py` from the files named in each section. Percentages refer to all generated tasks of the shared evaluation episodes unless stated otherwise.

Definitions:

- *Peer within 200 m*: at least one other vehicle lies within V2V range of the requester when the task is decided.
- *Candidate peer has a free core*: the peer that the environment preselects for the V2V action (`observation-space.md`) has at least one idle core. Because only the preselected peer is checked, this is a lower bound on the share of tasks with at least one eligible peer with spare capacity.
- *V2V share given a peer*: V2V decisions as a share of the tasks for which a peer is within range.
- *V2V tasks within deadline*: share of V2V-assigned tasks that meet their deadline.

The traffic quantities (peer within range, nearby vehicles) do not depend on the policy and are reported from the Greedy run; the candidate-peer state depends on earlier assignments and is reported for DQN-Full. Action proportions are calculated within each training seed and then averaged across the five runs. Greedy is deterministic and is reported as a single value. The audit replays the published evaluation and reproduces its deadline satisfaction exactly (control line).

## Executor-load audit

Source files: `results/load_audit/eval_executor_load_main_<period>.csv`, produced by `scripts/eval_executor_load.py`.

The audit is read-only and does not alter simulator behaviour. It records, for every task decision, the state of the candidate executors before the decision is applied. Aggregate deadline-satisfaction results reproduce the main evaluation exactly (control line). The audit covers the four targeted periods; it was not run for the morning ramp or the full-day sample.

Definitions:

- *Candidate-peer busy cores*: share of the preselected peer's cores that are occupied at decision time.
- *Candidate-peer backlog*: remaining work on the preselected peer's cores at decision time, in core-seconds (sum over cores of the time until each core becomes free).
- *Predicted peer wait*: waiting time before the task could start on the preselected peer, as computed by the environment's scheduler.
- The three candidate-peer quantities are averaged over all decisions at which a peer is available, whichever action is then taken.
- *V2V share of misses*: share of all deadline misses that occur on V2V-assigned tasks.
- *All executors loaded*: share of decisions at which every available candidate executor (requesting vehicle, preselected peer, nearest RSU) has a positive predicted wait.

Under load, Greedy assigns 42.5–44.1% of tasks to V2V, compared with 22.3–25.8% for DQN-Full, but its V2V deadline satisfaction is lower (41.7–54.9% against 56.7–65.0%). Approximately 60.7–66.2% of Greedy's misses occur on V2V assignments, compared with 35.9–36.1% for DQN-Full. Greedy's candidate peers are also more heavily loaded (66.2–86.5% busy cores against 53.4–74.3%). These observations locate the loss in V2V execution but do not independently identify the causal effects of workload, contention or spatial traffic configuration.

## Greedy-NoV2V

Source files: `results/greedy_nov2v/eval_greedy_nov2v_<main_period|general_24h>.csv`, produced by `scripts/eval_greedy_nov2v.py` on the same evaluation episodes as the main results. Greedy-NoV2V is Greedy with the V2V alternative removed.

| Period | Greedy | Greedy-NoV2V | Greedy-NoV2V − Greedy (pp) | DQN-Full − DQN-NoV2V (pp) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Night 00–04 | 0.708 | 0.719 | +1.0 | -0.1 |
| Ramp 04–08 | 0.644 | 0.637 | -0.8 | +10.4 |
| Evening 20–24 | 0.672 | 0.642 | -3.0 | +10.2 |
| Peak 08–09 | 0.639 | 0.634 | -0.5 | +10.7 |
| Peak 16–18 | 0.626 | 0.628 | +0.1 | +10.3 |
| Full day | 0.639 | 0.632 | -0.7 | +10.5 |

Removing V2V changes Greedy by −3.0 to +1.0 percentage points across the reported samples and by no more than 0.5 percentage points at the two peaks. The corresponding DQN-Full minus DQN-NoV2V difference is 10.2–10.7 percentage points under load. The V2V alternative therefore adds little to a rule that selects the executor with the earliest predicted finish, whereas the learned policy derives a large share of its advantage from it.
