# Evidence index for the Discussion

This document maps the interpretive claims in the Discussion to the detailed evidence released with the artefact.

## Operating-condition coverage

### Main-paper claim

The value associated with V2V availability appears under loaded conditions but not at night, and DQN-Full uses peer execution more selectively than Greedy.

### Supporting files

- `results/peer_audit/eval_peer_availability_main_<period>.csv`
- `results/load_audit/eval_executor_load_main_<period>.csv`
- complete peer-availability table ([`supplementary-service-analysis.md`](supplementary-service-analysis.md#full-peer-availability-table))
- Greedy-NoV2V comparison ([`supplementary-service-analysis.md`](supplementary-service-analysis.md#greedy-nov2v); `results/greedy_nov2v/`)
- morning-ramp robustness analysis ([`supplementary-service-analysis.md`](supplementary-service-analysis.md#morning-ramp-robustness-sample); `results/eval_main_04-08.csv`)

### Interpretation boundary

The evidence supports peer availability and selective use as explanatory mechanisms. It does not independently identify the causal contribution of peer availability because workload, density, spatial configuration and contention co-vary.

## State continuity

### Main-paper claim

Cold starts represent a different state trajectory, improve Greedy under loaded conditions and reduce the measured DQN-Full versus Greedy gap.

### Supporting files

- matched warm/cold window identifiers (`results/window-pairs.csv`)
- `results/peer_audit/eval_peer_availability_paired_<warm|cold>_<period>.csv`
- `results/load_audit/eval_executor_load_paired_<warm|cold>_<period>.csv`
- sampled-window density summaries (`data/paired/{warm,cold}_<period>/_stats.csv`)
- within-window density-recovery profiles (`results/paired_density/paired_density_frames.csv`)

The pair construction is described in [`warm-cold-protocol.md`](warm-cold-protocol.md) and the complete audit in [`supplementary-state-continuity.md`](supplementary-state-continuity.md#complete-paired-window-audit).

### Interpretation boundary

Cold starts are valid for explicit initialisation or recovery scenarios. They do not represent an already-running condition without evidence of adequate warm-up.

## Supplementary cold-training analysis

The cold-training study is exploratory and is not part of the principal RQ2 evidence.

Released material includes:

- warm- and cold-trained checkpoints (`checkpoints/berlin_general_*`, `checkpoints/berlin_coldtrain_*`);
- the cold-training table (`tab_coldtrain.tex`, generated by `analysis/make_tables.py`);
- training-frame density summaries (`results/coldtrain_density/coldtrain_density_frames.csv`);
- seed-level loaded-condition aggregates;
- Welch tests and confidence intervals;
- action and peer-availability audits (`results/peer_audit/eval_peer_availability_coldtrain_paired_<warm|cold>_<period>.csv`).

All are reported in [`supplementary-state-continuity.md`](supplementary-state-continuity.md#exploratory-cold-training-analysis), including the scope of the comparison with the earlier study. The comparisons are inconclusive at five trained instances per regime. No stable population-level cold-training effect is claimed.

## Accounting scope

### Main-paper claim

Requester and broader operational ledgers support opposite energy assessments of the same policy, and the tested reward scalarisation lowers requester energy without evidence of a loaded-condition system-energy saving.

### Supporting files

All in [`supplementary-energy-analysis.md`](supplementary-energy-analysis.md):

- complete condition-level energy ledgers;
- the reward table (`tab_reward.tex`, generated by `analysis/make_tables.py`);
- seed-level DQN-Energy results;
- action-share analysis;
- service-normalised energy;
- analytical energy break-even derivation.

### Interpretation boundary

The results concern modelled operational energy under the released parameters. They do not establish carbon or lifecycle impact, and alternative executor or communication efficiencies could change the break-even result.
