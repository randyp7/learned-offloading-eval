# Full-day traffic profile

`fig_diurnal.pdf` / `fig_diurnal.png` show the hourly mean traffic density (vehicles per non-empty frame) of the continuous 24-hour trajectory, the targeted operating periods and the cold-start densities at matched nominal times (one point per distinct start time evaluated in the matched warm/cold experiment, `data/paired/eval_sequence_<period>.csv`). The figure is generated by `analysis/make_tables.py`.

It is supplementary because the main paper reports the selected conditions in the Results tables and reports warm/cold density distortion directly in the RQ2 subsection.

The figure does not estimate a continuous causal density response. Density co-varies with workload, speed, spatial configuration, connectivity and peer availability.
